03 / Operating model · Recommended governance practice

The AI credits operating loop

Observations become decisions, not automatic purchase approvals. Each pass updates one shared record of evidence, ownership, and continuity risk.

Prose alternative. Observe alerts and anomalies; attribute consumption to users and accountable owners; forecast likely usage; let the responsible owner decide; apply authorized configuration or usage guidance; review actuals and business impact. Feed the outcome into the next observation cycle and retain evidence at every stage.

Three separate decisions. A forecast estimates demand. A configured cap determines enforcement. Authorization determines whether additional spending may proceed under your finance process. Raising a cap is not, by itself, a purchase authorization.

Source cadence. Cost owners review alerts daily; billing managers investigate spikes daily and report weekly. Review rollout patterns weekly, then monthly once stable. The owner chooses optimization, restriction, or a budget increase; the billing manager applies it. This visual is a synthesis, not a GitHub system execution trace.
