01 / Credit lifecycle · Copilot Business & Enterprise

From shared credits to metered usage

A shared included allowance is not a departmental wallet. Once it is exhausted, paid usage depends on policy and applicable enforced limits.

Prose alternative. Credit-consuming interactions are priced using the selected model and tokens. Business and Enterprise usage draws from the billing entity’s included pool first. When that pool is exhausted, the paid-usage policy determines whether usage can continue as metered charges; applicable enforced budgets can still block it.

Controls span the flow. The applicable user-level budget caps included and metered consumption and always enforces a hard stop. Cost-center included-usage controls can limit a group’s pool draw separately. This overview omits that early-overage path; see the guardrails diagram.

Reset, not rollover. Unused included credits expire at the monthly reset. Copilot access has no included pool and is metered from first use. Inline completions and next edit suggestions do not consume AI credits and remain available after budget blocks.
