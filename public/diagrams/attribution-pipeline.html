04 / Data flow · A recommended reporting pipeline

Attribute usage before optimizing spend

Keep credit consumption and monetary amounts distinct. Map the evidence to accountable cost owners before you restrict models or change budgets.

Prose alternative. The billing manager downloads AI usage data, preserves credits and USD measures, and joins usage with user and cost-center ownership. Cost owners validate the attribution and receive showback dashboards. Finance may then perform approved internal chargeback using agreed rules.

Keep the measures honest. The source report distinguishes quantity (credits), gross_amount, discount_amount, and net_amount (USD). Consumed credits are not automatically an additional cash charge: included usage and discounts matter. Do not equate gross usage value with the invoice.

Recommended data-quality gate. Keep unresolved identities visible rather than silently dropping them. Review attribution before using a dashboard to justify restrictions, financial transfers, or forecast adjustments. Showback creates visibility; chargeback is your internal finance process.
