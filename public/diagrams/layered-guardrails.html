02 / Control catalog · Where each boundary applies

Guardrails are not an allocation tree

These controls govern different consumption surfaces. Rows show scope and purpose—not a sequential evaluation order or inherited funding.

Prose alternative. ULBs cap each person across both phases. Within the ULB family, specificity is individual → cost-center ULB → universal; the most specific applies. A cost-center ULB remains a per-person limit, not the group’s metered budget.

Pool protection is separate. Cost-center included-usage controls cap the group’s draw using its license-funded allowance. At that cap, configured behavior can block members or move additional usage to paid overage, even before the enterprise pool is exhausted.

Make the continuity choice explicit. Metered budgets alert unless “Stop usage when budget limit is reached” is enabled. An applicable enforced limit can block usage despite remaining headroom elsewhere. Alert-only controls preserve continuity but do not cap charges. The Well-Architected recommendation favors alert-led enterprise monitoring; choose stops according to financial and interruption risk.
