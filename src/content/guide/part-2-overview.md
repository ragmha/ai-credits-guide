Knowing what a control does is not enough to decide which one an enterprise needs. Finance may require no additional charges while a workload owner expects uninterrupted service. A team may need fair access to shared credits while its existing budget only governs paid overage. Resolve the requirement before choosing a setting.

## What you will learn

- Ask the questions that distinguish personal consumption, included-pool fairness and additional spending.
- Agree on strict caps versus continuity with the people who own the consequences.
- Produce a decision brief with named approvers, responders and review dates.

The first chapter is a neutral needs assessment for administrators and people helping an enterprise plan. The second follows two teams sharing one pool and turns their requirements into an implementable brief. The summary gives you a final readiness check before rollout.
